Kilo Code / chat box

Closer actions, one clean chevron.

Three ways to tidy the Kilo chat box. In all three, Continue and Prompts sit close as a pair and every dropdown uses the same sharp chevron, centred on its label: Continue, the agent, the model, reasoning and Auto. Every dropdown below is clickable.

Today A · Quiet inline B · Segmented C · One summary
Today

What is wrong now

Continue and Prompts sit about 30px apart, so they do not read as a pair. The dropdown marks are 8px text characters that sit below the centre of the label, and two different shapes are used for the same job.

Proposed, 3x High
Variant A

Quiet inline

Smallest change

The layout you have now, cleaned up. Continue and Prompts sit 6px apart, and each dropdown gets the same chevron, 4px from its label and centred on it.

  • What changesSpacing and the chevron only. Nothing moves.
  • BehaviourThe chevron turns over while its menu is open, so the active control is obvious.
  • ProductivityNothing to relearn, and the smallest patch to the real chat box.
Variant B

Segmented controls

Controls that belong together share one outline. Continue becomes a split button: click the word to continue straight away, click the chevron for other ways to continue.

  • What changesAgent, model and reasoning join into one bar with hairline dividers. An up and down mark shows they are pickers.
  • BehaviourContinue is one click. Its menu is a separate 24px target, not a tiny mark.
  • ProductivityFewer missed clicks, and the groups read at a glance.
Variant C

One summary control

Agent, model and reasoning collapse into one line with one chevron. One panel changes all three in a single visit, and Continue is tinted as the likely next action.

  • What changesThree dropdowns become one. The bar carries two chevrons, not four.
  • BehaviourThe panel stays open while you pick, then Esc closes it.
  • ProductivityLeast clutter and the fastest full setup change. Changing only the model costs one more glance.
Reference

Rules shared by all three

Chevron
One inline SVG drawn at 12px, round caps, in the label colour. No text characters.
Alignment
Label and chevron are centred on one line inside a 26px control, 4px apart.
Spacing
Continue and Prompts are 6px apart. The row sits 8px above the message box.
Keyboard
Arrow keys move through a menu, Enter picks, Esc closes and returns focus to the control.
Colour
Controls stay quiet grey. Only the stop button and the open or selected state carry colour.
Sample content
Menu rows are placeholders to show behaviour, not a final list of options.
